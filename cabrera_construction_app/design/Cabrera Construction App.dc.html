How matching works
Matched: vendor, amount and date (within 3 days) agree with a receipt. The transaction takes the receipt's project.
Possible match: amount agrees but the vendor or date doesn't fully. Confirm or reject.
Needs project: no receipt uploaded, or the receipt has no project. Pick one here.
Deposits are matched to customer payments by amount and date.
What assigning does
Choosing a project for a transaction with no receipt adds it to that project's job expenses, flagged No receipt · from bank. It then appears in the project's reconciliation and in Business Expenses. Upload the receipt later and it links automatically.
