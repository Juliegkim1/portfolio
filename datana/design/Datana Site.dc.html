Five capabilities, one continuous path

Engagements usually span more than one. A dashboard is only as good as the pipeline beneath it, and a model is only as good as the question it was asked.

S.01

Data Strategy

A clear roadmap that ties data investment to business outcomes, so the next twelve months of work has a stated payback.

01Data maturity assessment and opportunity map
02Metrics and KPI framework tied to business goals
03Governance, ownership and data quality standards
04Tooling recommendations scoped to your budget
S.02

Data Engineering

Reliable pipelines and warehousing, so every team works from the same numbers and nobody maintains a private extract.

01ETL / ELT pipeline design and implementation
02Cloud data warehouse setup and migration
03API and third-party system integrations
04Data quality monitoring and documentation
S.03

Analytics & BI

Dashboards and reporting built around the decisions your team makes each week, not around what the tool renders by default.

01Executive and operational dashboards
02Self-serve reporting for non-technical teams
03BI platform implementation and rollout
04Ad-hoc analysis and deep-dive reporting
S.04

Predictive Modelling

Models that quantify what happens next and hand the answer to a person. The output is a forecast or a score; the decision stays yours.

01Demand and revenue forecasting
02Churn, risk and propensity scoring
03Validation against historical outcomes
04Model monitoring and retraining pipelines
S.05

AI Agents

We develop agents that work inside your business processes, carrying out the routine operational steps end to end so your team spends its time on the exceptions.

01Workflow mapping to find where an agent pays back
02Agents built into the tools your team already uses
03Human approval on the steps that carry real risk
04Guardrails, audit trails and ongoing tuning in production
