Design document · v1 · Sept 2026

datanasolutions.com

This is the design spec for the six-page consulting site. The site sells engineering credibility, so it leads with specifics and proof. Type and rules carry the structure, and the accent appears only as a thin line.

01 · Principles
  1. Specifics over reassurance
    Numbers, timelines and named outcomes. Remove any sentence that only makes the reader feel better.
  2. One action per page
    Home, Services, Client work and About each have one CTA block. Contact and the legal pages have none.
  3. Accent as a line
    Magenta is used for the filled CTA, rule markers and mono labels, and nowhere else.
  4. Structure through rules
    Grids are separated by hairlines. No shadows, no gradients, no floating cards.

IBM Plex Sans carries headings and text. IBM Plex Mono carries every number, label, index and piece of metadata. Headings never go above weight 500. Hierarchy comes from size and space.
